Frequently Asked Question

Rustomjee Balmoral at Golf Links, Chembur isn’t just a home it’s a rare sanctuary. Overlooking the iconic Bombay Presidency Golf Club, these limitededition residences are crafted to offer you serene views, spacious living, and world class amenities. Discover stillness, elegance, and a lifestyle curated for those who seek refined luxury.

The project features spacious 3, 4, and 5 bedroom residences with open plan layouts, full air-conditioning, elegant sundecks, and dedicated staff quarters. Each floor has just two homes serviced by three elevators, ensuring maximum privacy.

Residents enjoy access to a central landscaped courtyard, themed outdoor zones (Forest, Grove, Meadow, etc.), a fully equipped clubhouse, fitness studio, yoga spaces, pools, children’s play areas, BBQ pavilions, and work-from-garden pods.

The community features a multi tier security system, gated entry, and restricted floor access. With only two homes per floor and thoughtfully designed pathways that separate pedestrian and vehicle movement, privacy and peace of mind are top priorities.

Ideally located in Chembur, the property offers excellent connectivity just 15 minutes to BKC, 20 minutes to Domestic Airport, and 30 minutes to Colaba. It’s also well connected to Navi Mumbai, Ghatkopar, and South Mumbai via major highways and connectors.

Rustomjee Balmoral is designed by renowned architect Hafeez Contractor and principal designer Patty Mak, with landscape design by COEN Design International, Singapore. Together, they have created a harmonious blend of architecture and nature, offering a truly elevated living experience.
