Rustomjee 180 Bayview has 2 BHK residences (824 sq. ft. to 840 sq. ft.), 3 BHK residences (1221 sq. ft. to 1407 sq. ft.), and 4 BHK residences (1646 sq. ft. to 1898 sq. ft.) available.

Rustomjee 180 Bayview is located in a quiet bylane in Matunga (W), Mumbai.

Rustomjee 180 Bayview has apartments available in three sizes: 2 BHK (824 sq. ft. to 840 sq. ft.), 3 BHK (1221 sq. ft. to 1407 sq. ft.), and 4 BHK (1646 sq. ft. to 1898 sq. ft.).
