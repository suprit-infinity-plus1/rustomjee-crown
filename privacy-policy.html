Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to https://rustomjee-mumbai.in

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide while using this Website.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: When you fill out enquiry forms or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, and project preferences.
  • Automatic data: Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited for improving user experience.
  • Third-party data: We may receive information through advertising platforms or analytics tools that help us understand visitor interests.

How We Use the Information

  • To respond to your property enquiries and provide relevant project details.
  • To send you updates or promotional materials about Rustomjee projects you have shown interest in (only with consent).
  • To improve our website functionality and marketing communication.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Information Sharing

Your information may be shared with the official Rustomjee sales team or its authorized representatives solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or assisting with project information. We do not sell or rent your data to unrelated third parties.

Cookies

This Website may use cookies and tracking tools to enhance your browsing experience. You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to secure your personal information. However, please note that transmission of information over the internet is not completely secure.

Third-Party Links

This Website may include links to third-party project sites or social media pages. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those external websites.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with the revised date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your data, please reach out through the contact form on our website.
